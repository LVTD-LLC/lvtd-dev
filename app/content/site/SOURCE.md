# Content provenance

The HTML and blog content were imported from the public https://lvtd.dev website
on 10 November 2026. This is a preview of a replacement website, operated by the
same owner. Content follows the live sitemap plus public Jev question links.
Production classes, inline styles, JavaScript, and checkout forms were removed;
the generated ShipRust layout supplies the design. Project images are copied
from LVTD-LLC/lvtd-site's public assets.

The benchmark is a read-only snapshot. `data.json` retains the live export's
metadata; `methodology.md` retains its documented interpretation and limitations.
No new inference or paid provider requests run in this preview.
